FAQs
Why is my package delayed?
Delays can occur due to high shipment volumes, weather disruptions, customs clearance, or an incorrect delivery address. You can check the latest status using your tracking number above. If your shipment is delayed beyond the expected date, please contact our support team.
What do I do if the tracking page doesn't show the expected date of arrival?
Tracking information is updated at each checkpoint and may take up to 24 hours to reflect the latest status. If your expected delivery date is not showing, ensure your tracking number is entered correctly. If the issue persists, reach out to our Support Center.
How can I get the contact number of my delivery partner?
Once your shipment is out for delivery, the assigned delivery partner's contact number is shared via SMS and email to the registered contact on record. You can also find this on the tracking result page once your package is dispatched to a local hub.
What should I do if I am not available at the time of delivery?
If you miss a delivery attempt, our partner will try again on the next business day. You can also use the tracking page to request a safe-drop, re-schedule delivery, or redirect your package to a SwiftRoute pickup point near you.
My shipment has been returned / cancelled. What should I do?
A return is initiated if delivery attempts are exhausted or if the package was refused. Please contact the original seller or merchant to initiate a re-dispatch or refund. SwiftRoute does not process returns directly — these are managed by the shipper.
How do I file a claim for a lost or damaged shipment?
Claims for lost or damaged shipments must be filed within 7 days of the expected delivery date. Navigate to Support → File a Claim and provide your tracking number, order details, and photos of any visible damage. Our team will respond within 2 business days.
Need Help?

Our team is ready to assist you with any shipment queries, available around the clock. You can reach us at any time for immediate assistance. We will get back to you as soon as possible.
